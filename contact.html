Make a referral

Start with
the basics.

We’ll confirm fit and availability and help coordinate the next steps.

Email a referral inquiry

Helpful information to discuss

  • The service requested: Day SKYS, Night SKYS, or Family Support
  • The youth’s needs, strengths, and education or employment goals
  • Preferred start date and scheduling needs
  • Referral requirements and supervision expectations
  • Transportation needs and approved pickup and drop-off locations
  • Any safety concerns or existing crisis planning needs

For an initial email, include your contact information and a brief overview. We can coordinate how to share detailed referral information.

What happens next?

We talk through the referral, confirm availability, and arrange intake with the youth and caregiver. Together with the referring team, we establish goals, scheduling, and expectations before services begin.
