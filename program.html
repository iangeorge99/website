Shared activity blocks

What time at SKYS can include.

Day SKYS and Night SKYS use similar activity blocks. Activities, order, and time spent vary based on each youth’s needs, school schedule, and referral goals.

01

Check-in & planning

Talk through how things are going, upcoming responsibilities, and goals for the session.

02

Schoolwork & organization

Work on assignments, plan ahead, and build habits that support school engagement and follow-through.

03

Job readiness & life skills

Practice interview skills, work on applications and résumés, or take steps toward IDs, permits, and daily living goals.

04

Lunch / dinner

Share a meal and take time to connect, practice conversation, and build everyday routines.

05

Recreation & community activities

Take part in games, physical activity, and approved outings that build relationships, social skills, and decision-making.

06

Wrap-up & next steps

Review progress, recognize effort, and make a plan for what comes next.

Community activities are selected to fit each youth’s needs and applicable supervision requirements.

At home & in the community

Family Support

Individualized support for youth and caregivers.

We help families work through barriers and take practical steps toward education, employment, and daily living goals.

  • Hands-on help with schoolwork, organization, and employment goals
  • Work with caregivers to identify barriers and develop positive solutions
  • Build communication, healthy relationships, and positive social skills
  • Practice coping skills, routines, boundaries, and follow-through
  • Help connect families with community resources and essential documents

Services are scheduled around family availability, including day, evening, and weekend appointments, and coordinated with the referring team.

Service planning

Intake assessment is completed within 7 days of accepting the referral, with a written service plan developed within 10 days of intake. Ongoing coordination with probation supports agreed goals.

Reporting program

Youth ages 12–18 on juvenile probation. Up to 6 youth per staff member, with services planned around individual needs and referral goals.

Transportation

Transportation to and from the reporting center is provided within a 30-mile radius and coordinated during referral and intake.

Working together

We ask youth to participate, communicate, and practice the skills they are learning. Caregivers help identify what is working and where more support is needed.

Communication & accountability

We coordinate with families and supervising teams, document attendance and participation, and provide updates on goals. When progress stalls, we adjust the plan and communicate the next steps.

Planning and supervision details

Intake includes the youth and caregiver, with an individualized schedule or service plan aligned to referral goals. Crisis and safety planning is developed and updated as needed.

If a youth leaves after arriving and cannot be located, we follow required notification procedures and document efforts. Details are coordinated with the assigned supervising officer during referral and intake.
