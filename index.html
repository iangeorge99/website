Our approach

Trust is where
progress begins.

We take time to get to know each youth, including their interests, goals, and what gets in their way. Through consistent support and shared activities, we build relationships that make it easier to talk openly, work through challenges, and accept guidance.

We use that trust to reinforce accountability, connect youth with positive outlets, and help them make progress at school and toward employment.

Finding the right fit

When SKYS may
be a good fit.

A youth may benefit from SKYS when they need support with:

  • Staying engaged in school and following through on assignments
  • Taking the next steps toward a job or greater independence
  • Building routines and positive relationships
  • Finding constructive activities and making use of free time
  • Working through challenges at home with caregiver support

We’ll talk through the youth’s needs, referral requirements, and availability to help determine whether our services are a good fit.
